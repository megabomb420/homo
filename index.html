FIELD NOTES / 001

A story told in
bone and light.

HOMO is a poetic reconstruction, not a museum diagram. Seven species stand in for a family tree that is really a braided river: branches that met, diverged, and sometimes disappeared. The figure in the centre is one continuous sculpture, re-proportioned in real time as you move through deep time.

HOW TO MOVE

Scroll, drag the scrollbar, or use the chapter rail. Move your pointer to shift the air around the scene.

WHAT YOU HEAR

Wind, a low drone and a fire synthesised in the browser. Sound is off until you ask for it.

THE SCIENCE

Dates and brain volumes are rounded and debated. “Earliest known” is doing a lot of work in this story.

THE CRAFT

Procedural bone anatomy, hand-written shaders, particles, bloom and a scroll timeline. Built with Three.js.
